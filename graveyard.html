The Graveyard

A quantitative researcher is judged not by the strategies they trade, but by the strategies they have the discipline to throw away. Here are the strategies that almost made it.

NQ 1m 3-Red Candle Scalp

Close-to-Success: 63.5% Win Rate.

Fatal Flaw: Cost-to-Risk Ratio. Median risk 1.50 pts. Cost 1.0 pt. Cost 66% of risk. Expected Value negative. Wiped to $0.

NAS100 Staggered Overnight

Close-to-Success: Generated +21,300 gross points.

Fatal Flaw: Max Drawdown. Overlapping 14 entries multiplied exposure, creating a -32,026 point Max Drawdown.

N1: NAS100 30-Min ORB

Close-to-Success: 71.5% Continuation Rate.

Fatal Flaw: Whipsaw. The "continuation rate" only measured daily close. Stop at opposite OR boundary resulted in 22.3% WR and -79% DD.

G6: XAU Keltner Mean Reversion

Close-to-Success: Vendor claimed 53.3% WR and PF 2.79.

Fatal Flaw: Falling Knives. Triggering on a wick touch resulted in a 28.6% win rate and -100% CAGR. Needs a trend filter.

NQ 1m Streak Fade

Close-to-Success: 70.1% Win Rate.

Fatal Flaw: Risk-Reward Math. TP 20 pts, SL 60 pts. 70% WR not enough to overcome 1:3 RR. PF was 0.88.

Zeiierman S/R Profile

Close-to-Success: Visually flawless S/R levels on TradingView.

Fatal Flaw: Over-trading chop. Triggering on every volume pivot resulted in 26,000 trades and 31.6% WR. Visual confirmation does not equal algorithmic edge.

Intraday Mean Reversion (High WR Trap)

Close-to-Success: 78.1% Win Rate (Looked incredible).

Fatal Flaw: RR < 1 Math. Risked 2.0 ATR to make 0.5 ATR. Even winning 8 out of 10 times, the 2 losses wiped out the profit. PF 0.93.

AOX Model (Negative Fib Extensions)

Close-to-Success: Deep application of ICT PO3 and SMT concepts.

Fatal Flaw: Gross edge was 1.05 PF. Once a $0.30 spread was applied, the 30,000 trades bled out to a 0.88 PF. The limits are too close to the sweep; transaction costs eat the reversal.

Fibonacci Limit Fade (Sweeps/News)

Close-to-Success: Beautiful theory of fading algorithmic overreactions.

Fatal Flaw: Stepping in front of momentum. Placing limits at 2.25/4.25 extensions resulted in a 1.3% Win Rate because the market continues trending. Even 0.5 retracements failed 14.5.
