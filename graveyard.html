The Graveyard

A quantitative researcher is judged not by the strategies they trade, but by the strategies they have the discipline to throw away. Here are the strategies that almost made it.

NQ 1m 3-Red Candle Scalp

Close-to-Success: 63.5% Win Rate.

Fatal Flaw: Cost-to-Risk Ratio. Median risk was 1.50 pts. Cost was 1.0 pt. Cost was 66% of the risk unit. Expected Value was deeply negative. Wiped to $0.

NAS100 Staggered Overnight

Close-to-Success: Generated +21,300 gross points.

Fatal Flaw: Max Drawdown. Overlapping 14 entries multiplied exposure to the same intraday swings, creating a -32,026 point Max Drawdown.

N1: NAS100 30-Min ORB

Close-to-Success: 71.5% Continuation Rate.

Fatal Flaw: Whipsaw. The "continuation rate" only measured daily close, not path. Placing the stop at the opposite OR boundary resulted in a 22.3% win rate and -79% DD.

G6: XAU Keltner Mean Reversion

Close-to-Success: Vendor claimed 53.3% WR and PF 2.79.

Fatal Flaw: Falling Knives. Triggering on a wick touch of the Keltner channel resulted in a 28.6% win rate and -100% CAGR. Needs a trend filter to survive.

NQ 1m Streak Fade

Close-to-Success: 70.1% Win Rate.

Fatal Flaw: Risk-Reward Math. The TP was 20 pts, SL was 60 pts. The 70% win rate was not high enough to overcome the 1:3 risk-to-reward ratio. PF was 0.88.

ICT Low of Day (LOD) Reversal

Close-to-Success: 28.4% Prop Pass Rate (Highest of early tests).

Fatal Flaw: Stop Width. The 1-minute structural stop was 40 points wide. On a $5k account, one contract risks $800, instantly blowing the $250 daily loss limit.

Video Turtle Variant

Close-to-Success: 31% CAGR claimed by the strategy video.

Fatal Flaw: Over-Leverage. Reverse-engineering the math revealed it was just 6x leverage on standard daily Turtle. Blows the 10% prop drawdown limit immediately.

Volatility Rotation

Close-to-Success: Volatility persistence is real (rho 0.82).

Fatal Flaw: Misguided Logic. Trading whatever asset had the highest volatility last year. The Oracle test (perfect hindsight) still lost money. Volatility dictates swing size, not direction.

Zeiierman S/R Profile

Close-to-Success: Visually flawless support/resistance levels on TradingView.

Fatal Flaw: Over-trading the chop. Triggering on every volume pivot resulted in 26,000 trades with a 31.6% win rate. The 33.3% break-even threshold was never crossed after costs. Visual confirmation does not equal algorithmic edge.
