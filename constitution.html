THE CONSTITUTION

The 12 Rules. No exceptions. Conviction is a liability.

"You will become a successful trader when you are more afraid of not following your rules than you are of losing money." — Unknown
1. Split IS/OOS at 2014 or 2021.
2. Shift signals by 1 bar. Enter at NEXT bar open.
3. Apply realistic costs (2 bps FX, 3 pts NAS, 20 bps crypto).
4. Report Sharpe, CAGR, MaxDD, win rate, avg win/loss.
5. Include buy-and-hold reference.
6. Include random-direction baseline (200+ seeds).
7. Report IS/OOS separately. Flag OOS Sharpe > 0 AND |t| > 2.
8. Prop-firm: enforce daily loss, total loss, min days.
9. If too good, find the bug before celebrating.
10. No look-ahead. Ever.
11. Log every retired strategy as a case study.
12. No curve-fitting. Round numbers only.
