The Constitution.

This is not a get-rich-quick manual. It is the absolute rulebook written in the blood of blown prop accounts. Conviction is not an edge—it is a liability. These are the strategies that survived the 12 Rules.

01. The Genesis of the 12 Rules

For years, I traded like a retail gambler: chasing YouTube indicators, trusting signals, and jumping into prop-firm challenges blind. I was looking for a holy grail. Instead, I found the graveyard.

The turning point was realizing that the market doesn't care about my conviction. It only cares about data, probability, and transaction costs. I stripped everything down and built a 12-rule methodology. Rule 1 is No Look-Ahead. Rule 2 is Shift Signals by 1 Bar. We test, we validate, and if it fails the Out-of-Sample split, we throw it in the fire.

PROP VIABLE (MARGINAL)

BENCHED
