Overview

Ultra Edge is an independent research entity founded in 2021. We specialize in the rigorous validation of systematic trading strategies across FX, indices, and digital assets.

Operational Infrastructure

All research is conducted using a proprietary pipeline built in Python. We maintain our own databases of 1-minute and higher-timeframe data for all traded assets, ensuring complete control over data integrity and eliminating reliance on third-party charting platforms.

Governance

The research process is governed by the 12 Rules of the Ultra Edge Protocol. No strategy is deployed or referenced in portfolio materials without passing strict out-of-sample validation and realistic cost modeling.
