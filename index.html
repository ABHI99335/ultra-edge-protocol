HOW I THINK

My journey began as a retail trader trusting indicators like RSI and MACD. It was noise dressed as signal. The losses in prop-firm accounts served as structured tuition, forcing me to pivot into quantitative finance and order flow.

Today, I do not deploy capital based on unverified strategies. The research process is entirely systematic. If an edge cannot survive strict In-Sample/Out-of-Sample splits and realistic transaction costs, it is discarded.

Falsifiability over conviction. Every hypothesis is treated as false until the data proves otherwise. I do not trade stories; I trade statistical edges that have survived stress-testing.
