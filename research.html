Post-Mortems (The Graveyard)

These strategies looked profitable at first glance but failed strict validation.

NQ 1m 3-Red Candle Scalp

FATAL FLAW: Cost-to-Risk Ratio.

The median risk was 1.50 points. The round-trip cost was 1.0 point. The cost was 66% of the risk unit. Even with a 63.5% win rate, the Expected Value was deeply negative. Wiped to $0.

NAS100 Staggered Overnight

FATAL FLAW: Max Drawdown.

Buying hourly from 10am to 11pm ET created a -32,026 point Max Drawdown. Overlapping 14 entries multiplies exposure to the exact same intraday swings.

ICT Low of Day (LOD)

FATAL FLAW: Stop Width.

The 1-minute structural stop is 40 points wide. On a $5k account, one standard contract risks $800, instantly blowing the $250 daily loss limit (62% fail rate).

120-Variation Pullback Grid

FATAL FLAW: Ruinous Drawdown.

The optimizer found a +30% CAGR variation, but it came with a -72% Max Drawdown. Optimization without a Calmar constraint is just a slow path to blowing up.

Rejected Alternative Data

  • Weather & Lunar Cycles: Appeared to have a +15 bps edge in-sample. Split-half flipped sign. Pure noise.
  • Google Trends: Searches for "recession" lagged the market by exactly 3 weeks. It is a reaction effect, not a prediction effect.
