The Fringe Data Graveyard (Sandbox Multiverse)

We tested dozens of alternative data sets in the Sandbox. We subjected them to the 3-Stage Factor Validation (Correlation -> Lagged Correlation -> Split-Half). Every single one failed. Fringe data is not orthogonal; it correlates with markets only through shared macro causes. We do not trade it.

Weather & Lunar Cycles

FAILED EXPERIMENT

Google Trends & Wikipedia
