The Fringe Data Graveyard (Sandbox Multiverse)

We tested dozens of alternative data sets in the Sandbox. We subjected them to the 3-Stage Factor Validation (Correlation -> Lagged Correlation -> Split-Half). Every single one failed. Fringe data is not orthogonal; it correlates with markets only through shared macro causes. We do not trade it.

FAILED EXPERIMENT

Weather & Lunar Cycles

Tested cloud cover in Chicago and lunar phases against NAS100. Appeared to have a +15 bps edge in-sample. Result: Split-half flipped sign. Pure curve-fitting noise.

FAILED EXPERIMENT

Google Trends & Wikipedia

Searched for spikes in "recession" and "bankruptcy". Result: They lagged the market by exactly 3 weeks. It is a reaction effect, not a prediction effect.

FAILED EXPERIMENT

Gun Sales & Weather

Classic alternative data. Correlated perfectly with volatility in 2020. Result: Once we normalized for the COVID regime shift, the correlation disappeared entirely.

FAILED EXPERIMENT

Cross-Asset Correlation

Tested if Bitcoin leads NAS100. Result: Correlation of 0.45 on same-day, but 0.00 on lagged day. They move together due to liquidity, not causation.
