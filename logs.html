The Graveyard &
The Breakthroughs.

A quantitative researcher is judged not by the strategies they trade, but by the strategies they have the discipline to throw away. We have tested hundreds of variations over millions of bars. Here is the autopsy of our failures.

The Illusion of Intraday Scalping

We spent months trying to build the perfect 1-minute scalping engine on NQ futures. We tested 3-red-candle pullbacks, 120-variation grid searches, and structural stops. We found a brutal truth: Cost exceeds edge at retail.

NQ 1m 3-Red Candle Scalp

Cause of Death: Spread Drag

The median risk was 1.50 points. The round-trip cost was 1.0 point. The cost was 66% of the risk unit. Even with a 63.5% win rate, the Expected Value was deeply negative. Account wiped to $0.

120-Variation Pullback Grid

Cause of Death: Ruinous Drawdown

The optimizer found a +30% CAGR variation on XAU 5m. But it came with a -72% Max Drawdown. Optimization without a Calmar constraint is just curve-fitting into an early grave.

Volatility Rotation

Cause of Death: Misguided Logic

The idea: trade the asset with the highest volatility last year. The Oracle test (perfect hindsight) still lost money. Volatility dictates the size of the swings, not the direction. High volatility without an edge is just moving toward bankruptcy at 100 mph.

NAS100 Staggered Overnight

Cause of Death: Correlation Trap

Buying hourly from 10am to 11pm ET. It generated +21,300 gross points, but the Max Drawdown was -32,026 points. Overlapping 14 entries does not diversify risk; it multiplies exposure to the same intraday swings.

The Lessons That Built The Godfather

Every card in the graveyard above forced a rewrite of the Master Playbook. We learned that you cannot scalp with tight structural stops on a 1-minute chart. We learned that staggering positions creates a false sense of diversification. We learned that pure price-action ORBs and MA crossovers are mathematically dead.

We stopped looking for a holy grail and started looking for structural arbitrage—mispricings in time and liquidity. This shift led us to the Turtle Breakouts and the Portfolio engines.

The goldmine isn't the winning strategies. The goldmine is the data that proves what doesn't work.
